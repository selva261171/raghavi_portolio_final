Safety awareness & safety culture

A Study on Safety Awareness and Safety Culture among Employees
Ashok Leyland Ltd., Plant I, Hosur · MBA Summer Internship Project · Jun–Jul 2026

Purpose: To evaluate safety awareness and safety culture among employees, and analyze how safety communication, training, and management commitment influence employee safety performance in a large Indian commercial vehicle manufacturing environment.

Research design
Descriptive Research Design
Sampling method
Convenience Sampling — Census Survey
Data collection
Structured questionnaire and direct survey
Sample size
549 respondents

This study examines the relationship between safety awareness, safety culture, and employee safety performance at Ashok Leyland Plant 1, Hosur. The research investigates how safety communication, safety training, and management commitment collectively influence employee safety outcomes in a large-scale commercial vehicle manufacturing environment.

CavinKare strategic analysis

Challenges of Sustaining Growth and Expanding Business
MBA Case Study · Team Project

Strategic analysis of CavinKare Pvt. Ltd., an Indian FMCG company established in 1983 by Mr. C.K. Ranganathan. The case study examined how CavinKare can sustain rapid growth while competing with multinational FMCG companies such as Hindustan Unilever and Procter & Gamble.
